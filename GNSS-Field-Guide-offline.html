Mapping when GPS can't be trusted

Satellite positioning is jammed or faked in more and more places, especially near conflict zones. This guide helps mappers notice it, keep their data correct and stay safe, and helps project managers find affected submissions.

What is happening

Jamming

A strong radio signal drowns out the satellites. Your phone loses its position or reports it very inaccurately.

    Spoofing

    Fake satellite signals tell your phone it is somewhere else. The position often looks precise and stable, which makes spoofing harder to notice than jamming.

      Ordinary phones cannot reliably prove that a position is genuine. Treat any position that does not match what you see around you as wrong, even when the app shows good accuracy.
